# SUN1992 — the 11-variable catch, resolved

## What Sun 1992 actually is
Zhi-Wei Sun announced (1992, Sci. China Ser. A 35(1992) 257–269, "Reduction of unknowns in
Diophantine representations") and published IN FULL only in 2021 (arXiv:1704.03504 → Sci. China
Math. 64(2021) 281–306, "Further results on Hilbert's tenth problem"; PhD thesis 1992) the
**11 Unknowns Theorem**:

> For any r.e. set A ⊆ N, there is a polynomial Q_A(z₀,…,z₁₁) ∈ Z[z₀,…,z₁₁] such that for any
> a ∈ N: a ∈ A ⟺ ∃z₁…∃z₁₁ [Q_A(a,z₁,…,z₁₁) = 0], with all variables ranging over **Z**.
> Consequently ∃¹¹ over Z is undecidable (deg Q_A can be taken < 8.1142×10⁴⁶).

(MRDP-type universal-pair improvement of Jones' (58,4) / 9-unknowns; the "11 vars for Z" in Jones'
trade-off table = best witness bound for undecidable systems over the integers.)

## The catch, precisely (three independent disqualifiers)
1. **Witnesses are integers, not rationals.** The theorem is about undecidability of ∃¹¹ over Z.
   Read with Q-valued variables, the solution set enlarges — the ⟺ against A is simply false in
   general. Nothing transfers to rational points.
2. **It is a per-set (universal) construction, not a definition of Z.** Each r.e. A gets its own
   Q_A. Applied to A = Z (or N): "N ⊆ N is Diophantine" is witnessed by the zero polynomial with
   ZERO variables — the 11 is a bound for UNDECIDABLE sets, and `Z ⊆ Z` is trivial. Board metric
   (rk^∃_Q(Z)+1, Daans–Dittmann–Fehm sense) measures something else entirely.
3. **The board's question — Z ⊆ Q — is untouched and expected NEGATIVE.** Whether Z is
   existentially definable in Q is open (Koenigsmann, Ann. Math. 183 (2016) 73–93, §1: "it is
   still open whether Z is existentially definable in Q"); Koenigsmann Cor 23: assuming strong
   Bombieri–Lang, NO infinite subset of Z is existentially definable in Q; Mazur's conjecture
   would forbid it (Mazur 1995 Astérisque 228). The OpenAI preprint (Oct 6 2026) itself declines
   to give "a single existential definition of Z in Q". So an unconditional 11-var (or ANY-var)
   definition of Z in Q would refute strong Bombieri–Lang + Mazur — the incumbents' construction
   is conditional precisely to avoid this.

## Consequence for the board
Sun's 11 is a **complexity frontier for MRDP over Z**, not a rival score. CONSTRUCTIONS.md §3 cites
it correctly as integer-unknown precedent for the quartic normal form lineage; the C4 refutation
("universal pairs encode r.e. sets of integers, not 'q ∈ Z inside Q'") stands. An "11-var Z-in-Q"
reading is a category error; nobody should spend a day on it.
